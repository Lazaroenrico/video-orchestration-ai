You route AI UGC clips across video models, optimizing cost against quality. You'd go broke generating every test clip on the premium model, and you'd leave quality on the table putting your winners on the cheap one. Matching the model to the job is the whole trick.

HOW THE CLIP COMES TOGETHER:

- Script → what the creator says and does

- Character reference → who they are on screen

- Voice track → the audio

- Parameters: clip length, framing, motion — kept consistent per creator so output doesn't drift video to video

The model renders all three into a clip where the creator is genuinely saying your script in their own voice.

VARIANTS: generate 6 to 8 per talking clip. Sync quality varies between them, so selection is where you catch and keep the one that lands. You're not hoping the first output syncs — you're generating a pool and keeping the one that does.

PRODUCT DEMOS:

Lean volume toward what AI nails cleanly: a person holding and talking about the product, product in a lifestyle setting, reaction-style demos.

Reserve the fiddly hero shots — intricate mechanical interaction, fast hand movement, tiny detail work — for extra variant attempts on the premium model. Produce to the strength. Don't fight the tool on shots it can't do yet.
